How it works

Four steps, from link to agent

No settings to learn. Paste a model, read the answer, and go.

01

Paste a model link, press Check

Paste any Hugging Face link or name, like unsloth/Qwen3-4B-GGUF. The app reads your CPU, RAM and graphics chip and answers for this PC.

  • A clear verdict: runs, tight, slower, or won't fit
  • Memory needed and the expected speed range
  • The file to download, picked for you
02

Preview the speed

Feel the speed before you spend the download: a sample answer is typed at the pace the model will reach on your machine.

  • One answer first: perfect, good, tight or slow
  • Switch versions and watch the pace change
03

Battle models, get one winner

Not sure which one? Put up to four models in a Battle. Each scores points for quality, speed, memory and context on your PC, and one wins.

  • Too-big models still fight, and lose with the reason
  • Download the winner in one click
04

Use it in any AI agent

Open Agents, pick a downloaded model and press Start. Copy the OpenAI or Anthropic address, the key and the model ID into your agent.

  • One local server, all on your PC
  • Long agent requests are trimmed to fit
Benefits

Stop downloading models that won't run

Most people find out a model is too big or too slow only after a 10 GB download. This app tells you first.

A clear answer

One verdict per model for your exact hardware, not a table to decode.

The right file

Q2, Q4, Q8, F16… the largest version that fits and still writes at reading pace is chosen for you.

Real numbers

Agents included

Private and offline

Any PC

          FAQ
          
Questions

        

        

          
Do I need a graphics card?
No. It works with NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics, and on the processor alone. It reads how much memory your graphics chip can really use, including chips that share system memory.

          
Does it send my data anywhere?
No. It talks to Hugging Face to list and download models and to GitHub to check for updates. Nothing about you is shared unless you press a Share button yourself.

          
How exact are the speeds?
Before a download they are estimates, shown as a range and corrected for your kind of graphics chip. After Measure speed they are real numbers from your PC, and other estimates are corrected by them.

          
Which models does it support?
GGUF models from Hugging Face, the format llama.cpp runs. For a model without GGUF files the app suggests GGUF copies made by others.

          
Is it really free?
Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence: free to use, change and share. No account, no ads, no tracking.
